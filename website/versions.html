1.0LatestSeptember 2026
  • Full-screen lyrics. Press ⌥⌘L and the song fills the screen, every line lighting up word by word.
  • Five scenes. Classic, Spotlight, Vinyl, Minimal and Shuffle.
  • Desktop widgets. Lyrics and Recently Played, in frosted Liquid Glass or solid cards.
  • Click a lyric to jump. Click any line in the Lyrics widget and the song jumps there.
  • Play it again. Click a song in Recently Played and it plays in the app it came from.
  • A player at the bottom of your screen. Shuffle, previous, play or pause, next, repeat and a progress bar you can drag, with waves that follow the music.
  • Fit Windows Between Widgets. ⌘⇧L resizes the front app into the space beside your widgets.
  • Share a lyric. Turn the lyric on screen into a card you can post.
  • Updates itself. Arioso checks once a day and offers to install new versions.
  • Spotify and Apple Music, with no account, no analytics and no ads.
