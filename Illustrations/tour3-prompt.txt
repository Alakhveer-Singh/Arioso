Replacement for Illustrations/tour3.jpg (used in the app's welcome tour, page 3, "Widgets
on Your Desktop", and on the website's "Desktop widgets" section).

Reference tour1.jpg and tour4.jpg for exact style/linework/tone.

Monochrome ink-pen illustration, same warm sepia/cream paper texture and crosshatching as
the companion pieces. Same person, headphones on, one hand raised and pointing at the
screen, sitting at the same night desk with the plant, mug, books and notebook.

The iMac screen shows TWO separate rounded-rectangle cards side by side, with the desktop
wallpaper visible faintly between them (not a full-bleed app window):
- Left card: a short vertical list titled "Recently Played" — four rows, each a small square
  album-cover thumbnail beside one or two handwritten squiggle-lines (song title + artist).
  Each row ends in a small timestamp mark. No icon of any kind at the row's end.
- Right card, taller: the lyrics widget — a square glowing album cover at the top, a thin
  progress line beneath it, then five or six handwritten squiggle-lines of lyrics below that,
  one bolder than the rest.
Below both cards, spanning the bottom edge of the screen, draw a slim pill-shaped bar — a
small square cover thumbnail on its left, one or two short squiggle-lines beside it, and
four tiny short vertical tick-marks on its right (a simple "sound wave" glyph, not musical
notes).

Do NOT include: any heart/like icon anywhere, any graph, chart, wavy line-plot, or numeric
readout, any "System" or stats card. Only the two widget cards and the slim bottom bar
described above.
